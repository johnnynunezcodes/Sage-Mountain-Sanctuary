# Verbal Branding

**Status: draft, inferred from the sanctuary's mission and audience — confirm and refine with the user.**

## Voice

Warm, welcoming, and grounded — not preachy or guilt-driven. Sage Mountain Sanctuary invites people in rather than lecturing them. Copy should feel like it's written by someone who genuinely loves these animals and wants to share that, not by an advocacy campaign.

## Tone guidelines

- Lead with the animals' individual stories and personalities, not statistics or shock value.
- Speak to plant-based living as an invitation ("come meet them and see for yourself") rather than a demand.
- Keep language simple and sensory — visitors are often coming for a peaceful, nature-connected experience (tours, yoga) as much as an educational one.
- Avoid graphic descriptions of animal suffering in general site copy; if rescue stories reference hardship, keep it brief and end on the animal's current wellbeing.
- Use the animals' names often — it's part of what makes the sanctuary feel personal.

## Audience

People interested in animal welfare, plant-based lifestyles, and spiritual/nature wellness (many visitors come first for yoga or a tour, not necessarily as existing vegans/vegetarians).
