# Sponsorship & Donation Policy

**Status: draft placeholder — no payment processor is connected yet. Do not publish as binding terms until reviewed and a real processor is chosen.**

## Program concept (as described by the user)

- Supporters can sponsor one specific animal, or multiple animals, at the sanctuary.
- Billing is a recurring subscription: monthly by default, with an every-two-weeks (bi-weekly) option.
- Sponsorships are managed on the `/sponsor` page of the website (currently UI-only — see `Website/README.md`).
