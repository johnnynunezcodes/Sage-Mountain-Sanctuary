## Mission

Sage Mountain Sanctuary gives rescued farm animals a safe, permanent home while inspiring visitors toward compassionate, plant-based living. Located about 10 miles from Park City, Utah, the sanctuary is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.

## What we do

- Provide lifelong sanctuary for rescued pigs, cows, chickens, goats, sheep, and geese (see the open question in the root `CLAUDE.md` about reconciling this species list with "turkeys").
- Run guided tours and educational programming so visitors can meet the animals and learn their individual stories.
- Host weekly yoga classes on the sanctuary grounds.
- Coordinate volunteer opportunities every Saturday.
- Promote plant-based, compassionate living through community engagement.

## Status

The sanctuary is currently at capacity and not accepting new animals.
