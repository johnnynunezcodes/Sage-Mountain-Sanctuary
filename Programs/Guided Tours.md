# Guided Tours

**Status: real schedule/pricing confirmed 2026-08-18 from the live site (sagemtn.org/tour). Rendered on `/visit/tour`.**

Guided tours let visitors meet the animals, hear their individual rescue stories, and learn more about Sage Mountain's mission. Located in Peoa, UT (about 10 miles from Park City).

- **Schedule**: seasonal — as of this writing, Wednesday, Saturday, and Sunday evenings at 5 PM. Confirm each season, since this changes.
- **Price**: $30/adult, $15/child.
- **Booking**: must be made online at least 48 hours before the tour date. No online booking is wired up on the new site yet — the live site books directly; the new site's `/visit/tour` page points visitors to `/about/contact` instead until a booking flow is built.
- **Cancellation/weather policy**: no refunds once booked (nonprofit, donation-reliant) — but Sage Mountain will reschedule for free if weather forces a cancellation.
