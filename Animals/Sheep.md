# Sheep

Real roster, sourced from the live site's "Sponsor our Animal Residents" page (sagemtn.org), saved 2026-08-18. Photos: `Website/public/images/animals/sheep/`.

## Jesse

- **Species**: Sheep
- **Photo**: `Website/public/images/animals/sheep/jesse.jpg`
- **Story**: Found as a baby in Cedar City, Utah, after his ears had been bitten off by dogs. The woman who rescued him bottle-fed him at home until he was ready to come to Sage Mountain.
- **Personality**: Greets guests every day, loves head scratches and napping in the sun.
- **Sponsorship**: $50.00/month.

## Martin

- **Species**: Sheep
- **Photo**: `Website/public/images/animals/sheep/martin.jpg`
- **Story**: Considered a "bummer" lamb the farmer had no interest in raising — a regular occurrence in the industry. The farmer chose to find him a new home instead, and that's how he came to live with us.
- **Personality**: Very curious — enjoys just being in your presence, hanging out with his sheep family, and any and all treats he can get.
- **Sponsorship**: $40.00/month.

## Sammie

- **Species**: Sheep
- **Photo**: `Website/public/images/animals/sheep/sammie.jpg`
- **Story**: Found high in the mountains, alone and very young, by a woman who snowshoed the area and returned daily to feed her through the winter. Once spring arrived, she was brought down by snowmobile to her new forever home at Sage Mountain.
- **Personality**: Very observant — enjoys hanging out with Martin and snacking on different plants.
- **Sponsorship**: $20.00/month.

## Ava

- **Species**: Sheep
- **Photo**: `Website/public/images/animals/sheep/ava.jpg`
- **Story**: Found abandoned with her brother Peter just four or five days after birth. Their caregivers bottle-fed them every few hours, and the babies became deeply attached to their new caregivers.
- **Personality**: A very gentle soul — enjoys head scratches and the company of her sheep and human friends.
- **Sponsorship**: $50.00/month.

## Peter

- **Species**: Sheep
- **Photo**: `Website/public/images/animals/sheep/peter.jpg`
- **Story**: Found abandoned with his sister Ava just four or five days after birth. Their caregivers bottle-fed them every few hours, and the babies became deeply attached to their new caregivers.
- **Personality**: Sees himself as the protector of all the sheep here — also loves expressing how he feels and eating sweet grain.
- **Sponsorship**: $50.00/month.
