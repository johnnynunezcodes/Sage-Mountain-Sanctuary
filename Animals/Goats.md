# Goats

Real roster, sourced from the live site's "Sponsor our Animal Residents" page (sagemtn.org), saved 2026-08-18. Photos: `Website/public/images/animals/goat/` (only Bruce has a confirmed photo so far).

## Jasper

- **Species**: Goat
- **Story**: Originally rescued by Haven Hollow Animal Refuge when the goat farm he lived at asked them to take him in, since his goat friends had grown old and passed away. When Haven Hollow later needed to rehome their residents, we were glad to take him in. He traveled from Idaho to Utah and quickly made friends with volunteers and Louis.
- **Personality**: Very curious, loves being involved in everything, and shows love through kisses and soft nibbles.
- **Sponsorship**: $30.00/month.

## Pam

- **Species**: Goat
- **Story**: Raised as part of a child's 4-H program. After forming a bond, the family didn't want her sent to auction and asked us to take her in along with her friends Bruce and Kevin — an inseparable trio.
- **Personality**: Enjoys watching the sunset, taking lots of naps, and stealing treats.
- **Sponsorship**: $50.00/month.

## Louis

- **Species**: Goat
- **Story**: Came to Sage Mountain with his brother Lenny after their previous owner sold their house and planned to send them to a livestock auction. We got word in time to negotiate their release to sanctuary.
- **Personality**: Loves hanging out with everyone who visits, leaning in for snuggles, and being the center of attention.
- **Sponsorship**: $30.00/month.
