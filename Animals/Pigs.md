# Pigs

Real roster, sourced from the live site's "Sponsor our Animal Residents" page (sagemtn.org), saved 2026-08-18. Photos: `Website/public/images/animals/pig/` (a few unattributed pig photos also live in `Website/public/images/animals/unsorted/`).

## Ralphy

- **Species**: Pig
- **Photo**: `Website/public/images/animals/pig/ralphy.jpg`
- **Story**: Found wandering the streets of South Jordan, likely a former backyard pet pig — a situation pet pigs are often raised into for slaughter. No one claimed him from animal control. Wasatch Wanderers fostered him temporarily, then Sage Mountain took him as a foster and, after a few months with no adopters, adopted him permanently.
- **Personality**: The sweetest little boy — loves belly rubs, meeting new people, and falling asleep to volunteers singing him lullabies (his favorite is "Twinkle Twinkle Little Pig").
- **Sponsorship**: $40.00/month.

## Harriet

- **Species**: Pig
- **Photo**: `Website/public/images/animals/pig/harriet.jpg` (a second photo, `harriet-smile.jpg`, is in `unsorted/`)
- **Story**: Came from a small Utah farm raising blonde-mangalitsa pigs for show, breeding, or meat. We learned of her situation March 27, 2022 via a Facebook post from a farmer looking to rehome or slaughter the pigs that day. She was found belly-deep in a muddy pool of feces, struggling to balance on a water bowl to reach clear space. Two months after her rescue she gave birth to four piglets.
- **Personality**: The first to find you for belly rubs and the last to leave your side. Loves exploring the pasture, feeding time, and bathing in the wallow.
- **Sponsorship**: $50.00/month.

## Morgan
