# Chickens

Real roster, sourced from the live site's "Sponsor our Animal Residents" page (sagemtn.org), saved 2026-08-18. Photos: `Website/public/images/animals/chicken/` (only Jeff Goldblum and Ariana Grande have confirmed photos so far; two unattributed group/coop photos are in `Website/public/images/animals/unsorted/`).

## Jeff Goldblum

- **Species**: Chicken
- **Photo**: `Website/public/images/animals/chicken/jeff-goldblum.jpeg`
- **Story**: Found on the side of the road with his sister Ariana — two tiny lives with an unknown past, bruised and frightened. A compassionate foster gave both siblings the safety and time they needed to heal before we were asked to give them a permanent home.
- **Sponsorship**: $10.00/month for 1 year.

## Penelope

- **Species**: Chicken
- **Story**: Found as a stray and ended up at the animal shelter; after her stray hold was up unclaimed, we brought her home to Sage Mountain.
- **Personality**: Was skittish on arrival, but after months of love and care learned to trust us. Now spends her time with the other hens, cuddling Joey the rooster every night, and saying hi to volunteers.
- **Sponsorship**: $15.00/month.

## Sally

- **Species**: Chicken
- **Story**: Also goes by Squishy. Found as a stray and ended up at the shelter unclaimed. Wasatch Wanderers picked her up and found a foster to care for her for two weeks before we brought her to Sage Mountain.
- **Personality**: Very comfortable with us from the start — Walter the rooster was quick to welcome and protect her. Enjoys exploring the sanctuary and getting to know her new family.
- **Sponsorship**: $15.00/month.

## Steve

- **Species**: Chicken
- **Story**: Originally kept as a pet hen with her sisters Piper and Stormy. When their caretakers had less time to care for them, they reached out so we could give the sisters a permanent, safe home together.
- **Personality**: Sweet — her caretakers thought she was a rooster when they named her, but she's a hen and kept the name. Enjoys time with her sisters and occasionally sitting on volunteers' laps.
- **Sponsorship**: $15.00/month.

## Piper

- **Species**: Chicken
- **Story**: Originally kept as a pet hen with her sisters Stormy and Steve. When their caretakers had less time to care for them, they reached out so we could give the sisters a permanent, safe home together.
- **Personality**: Sweet and very observant — enjoys time with her sisters, playing and napping in the bushes, and being hand fed.
- **Sponsorship**: $15.00/month.

## Walter

- **Species**: Chicken
- **Story**: Found wandering a neighborhood and ended up unclaimed at animal control. A friend heard about him and picked him up after his stray hold, then reached out to us.
- **Personality**: Fit right in with hen pals Ezekiel and Wednesday — loves protecting them and being louder than Joey the rooster. Won "Best Hair at Sage Mountain Sanctuary" this year.
- **Sponsorship**: $15.00/month.

## Delhi

- **Species**: Chicken
- **Story**: Named after the city — our newest resident at the time, found wandering a farm neighborhood by a friend with no owner ever identified. Worried about her fate if a neighboring farmer took her in instead, he reached out to us.
- **Personality**: Loves exploring the sanctuary with the other hens and Joey the rooster, and taking naps in the shade of the bushes.
- **Sponsorship**: $30.00/month.

## Abigail

- **Species**: Chicken
- **Story**: Originally raised as a backyard hen. A coyote attack claimed the lives of the other hens, leaving Abigail the sole survivor. Her caretaker reached out so she could join a new chicken family.
- **Personality**: Feisty — honed from defending her friends during the coyote attack — but friendly and sociable once at ease.
- **Sponsorship**: $20.00/month.

## Rosaline

- **Species**: Chicken
- **Story**: Came to us almost 3 years ago with her 3 sisters after their caretaker passed away and her son, who'd been watching them, reached out for help. Her sisters have since passed, but she's doing well and became close with Penelope.
- **Personality**: Enjoys time with Penelope, expressing herself with the cutest chirps, and relaxing near the other hens and Hei Hei the rooster.
- **Sponsorship**: $20.00/month.

## Ariana Grande

- **Species**: Chicken
- **Photo**: `Website/public/images/animals/chicken/ariana-grande.jpeg`
- **Story**: Found on the side of the road with her brother Jeff — two tiny lives with an unknown past, bruised and frightened. A compassionate foster gave both siblings the safety and time they needed to heal before we were asked to give them a permanent home.
- **Sponsorship**: $10.00/month.

## Ezekiel

- **Species**: Chicken
- **Story**: Raised in a dog run in someone's backyard to be eaten. When the family's health concerns forced them to give up her and her six siblings, Wasatch Wanderers reached out and we had space to take them in. They arrived covered in lice, underweight, and unwell; sadly one sibling passed shortly after rescue, but the rest recovered.
- **Personality**: Enjoys dust bathing, napping in the sun, and getting attention from volunteers.
- **Sponsorship**: $30.00/month.

## Wednesday

- **Species**: Chicken
- **Story**: Rescued from a backyard dog run where she was being raised to be eaten. After health issues forced her previous family to give her up, Wasatch Wanderers took her in and brought her to Sage Mountain. She's since needed surgery for severe osteomyelitis in one foot and complications in the other, and is recovering well at the sanctuary.
- **Personality**: Recovering and receiving the best care we can give her.
- **Sponsorship**: needs medical support — costs have totaled about $1,389 so far; not listed with a standard monthly sponsorship amount.
