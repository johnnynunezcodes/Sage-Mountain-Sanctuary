# Turkeys

Real roster, sourced from the live site's "Sponsor our Animal Residents" page (sagemtn.org), saved 2026-08-18. Photos: `Website/public/images/animals/turkey/` (Roxy doesn't have a confirmed photo yet).

## Moby

- **Species**: Turkey
- **Photo**: `Website/public/images/animals/turkey/moby.jpeg`
- **Story**: Rescued with the help of the nonprofit DxE (Direct Action Everywhere), who brought her and her sister Phoenix to safety from Sanpete County. She arrived timid, unsure, and frightened, but slowly healed with time and patience.
- **Personality**: Now one of the most affectionate and welcoming turkeys — adores greeting visitors and seeking out connection.
- **Sponsorship**: $20.00/month for 1 year.

## Brett

- **Species**: Turkey
- **Photo**: `Website/public/images/animals/turkey/brett.jpg`
- **Story**: Abandoned in a canyon near Nephi — a place often used for target practice — just days after New Year's. A compassionate individual spotted and took him in. Bullet casings were found in his feces after he arrived at Sage Mountain, underscoring the fate he narrowly escaped.
- **Personality**: Took on the role of protector for the other turkey hens; loves strutting around showing off his presence.
- **Sponsorship**: $50.00/month.

## Phoenix

- **Species**: Turkey
- **Photo**: `Website/public/images/animals/turkey/phoenix.jpeg`
- **Story**: Rescued with the help of the nonprofit DxE (Direct Action Everywhere), who brought her and her sister Moby to safety from Sanpete County. She arrived cautious and uncertain, but slowly learned this place was different — safe hands, soft voices, days without fear.
- **Personality**: A tender, affectionate presence — greets visitors sweetly and spends her days with her new family.
- **Sponsorship**: $20.00/month for 1 year.

## Roxy

- **Species**: Turkey
- **Story**: Found as a stray in Salt Lake City and unclaimed at the shelter. Wasatch Wanderers fostered her, then brought her to Sage Mountain to be a friend for Brett, who was looking for company at the time.
- **Personality**: The sweetest gal you'll meet — extremely social, vocal when she isn't being held, loves to snuggle and often falls asleep in laps. She's the alpha of the sanctuary's four turkeys.
- **Sponsorship**: $20.00/month for 1 year.
